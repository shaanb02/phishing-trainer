Quick Security Tips

1
Think Before You Click

Always pause and consider whether an email or link seems legitimate before clicking.

2
Verify the Sender

Check if the email address matches the claimed sender. When in doubt, contact them directly.

3
Hover Over Links

Hover your mouse over links to see the actual destination before clicking.

4
Report Suspicious Emails

Forward suspicious emails to your IT security team immediately.
